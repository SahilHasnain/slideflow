**I like the direction, but I would not target law students first.**

### What's good

* ✅ Real pain exists.
* ✅ Mobile-first users are underserved.
* ✅ Users need presentations regularly.

### What's risky

**Law students are a small niche.**

Ask yourself:

> **Do they make enough presentations to become repeat users?**

I'm not convinced.

### Better niche

Target **all college students**, then provide templates for specific fields.

Examples:

* Law
* Medical
* Engineering
* MBA
* BCA/MCA

### Better positioning

Don't build a PowerPoint editor.

Build:

> **"Create a presentation in 5 minutes on your phone."**

### MVP

* Choose subject
* Choose template
* Add headings
* Auto-generate slide structure
* Edit text
* Export to PPTX/PDF

### My rating

* Market: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
* Competition: ⭐⭐⭐
* Monetization: ⭐⭐⭐⭐
* MVP difficulty: ⭐⭐⭐
* Startup potential: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

**One concern:** This product becomes much stronger if AI is involved (for slide generation). Without AI, your main advantage must be **exceptional mobile UX** and high-quality templates.

**Overall:** I prefer this idea over a generic habit tracker, but I still think the **mobile programming learning platform** has higher long-term potential because users come back daily, while presentation creation is an occasional task.
