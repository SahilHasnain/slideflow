Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Slideflow is a mobile app that helps you create presentations using AI. Your privacy matters — this policy explains what data is handled and why.

Data Stored Locally

All presentation data — outlines, slides, templates, and exported files — is stored entirely on your device using local storage. Nothing is uploaded to a server or shared with anyone unless you explicitly export or share a file.

AI Feature

When you use the AI outline generation feature, your topic, audience, and tone inputs are sent to a Cloudflare Worker endpoint which forwards the request to a third-party AI provider. The data sent includes:

  • Topic / presentation title
  • Target audience description
  • Desired tone
  • The generated outline is sent back to your device only

This data is not logged, stored, or tracked on the worker or any intermediary service. Requests are stateless and discarded immediately after the AI response is returned.

Network Requests

Your device connects to the following endpoints when you use Slideflow:

  • AI endpoint — for outline generation (as described above)
  • Expo EAS services — standard Expo app functionality (authentication, updates)

No personal information beyond what is explicitly entered by you in the app is transmitted.

Analytics & Tracking

Slideflow does not collect analytics, usage metrics, crash data, advertising identifiers, or any form of user tracking. There are no analytics SDKs, ad networks, or third-party tracking scripts embedded in the app.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used is:

  • Cloudflare Workers — hosts the AI proxy endpoint
  • Expo / EAS — app infrastructure (builds, updates, authentication)

These services operate under their own privacy policies. Slideflow does not share any data with them beyond what is strictly required for their functionality.

Children's Privacy

Slideflow is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Reach out at GitHub Issues or contact the developer directly.
